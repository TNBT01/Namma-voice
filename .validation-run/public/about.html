About NAMMA VOICE

A student-focused platform for feedback, campus issue reporting, resolution tracking, and college resources.

What is Namma Voice?

NAMMA VOICE is a unified college web application developed for APS College of Arts and Science. It enables students to report campus infrastructure concerns, track resolution workflows in real-time, and download previous years' examination question papers.

Every reported issue is assigned a sequence tracking number (e.g. NV-2026-001) and routed directly to responsible campus departments (Maintenance, IT Department, Library, etc.).

Student services in one place

In academic environments, day-to-day equipment issues like classroom fan faults, computer lab network outages, drinking water cooler leaks, or library terminal downtime often take time to be addressed when reporting channels are informal or unrecorded.

NAMMA VOICE gives students a single place to submit concerns, follow their status, and access college academic materials.
