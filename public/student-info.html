Student Information & Guide

How students of APS College of Arts and Science submit concerns, track resolution timelines, and access academic question papers.

1. Purpose of the Namma Voice Platform

Namma Voice was established as a student-led initiative to create a direct, transparent channel between enrolled students and the college administration. Instead of issues being forgotten on paper notes or informal complaints, every reported concern receives an official, sequential tracking number (e.g. NV-2026-001).

Administrators review submitted concerns on an oversight dashboard, assign them to the appropriate campus department (such as Maintenance, IT Department, or Library), and log official progress remarks until physical resolution is verified.

2. Step-by-Step Reporting Procedure

  1. Register Your Account: Enroll using your name, college email, department (BCA, B.Sc, or B.A), and current semester. A unique student ID (e.g. APS-BCA-2026-042) is generated.
  2. Raise a Concern: Go to Raise Concern. Select the applicable facility category, specify the precise room or laboratory location, describe the problem clearly, and attach optional photo evidence.
  3. Track Progress in Real Time: Monitor your complaint from My Complaints. You can inspect whether the issue is Pending, In Progress, or Resolved.
  4. Review Administrative Remarks: When maintenance technicians or faculty inspect the issue, official remarks appear in your complaint's chronological timeline.

3. Guidelines for Constructive Issue Reporting

  • Be specific with location details (e.g. "Room 304, 3rd Floor Arts Block, Fan 2" rather than simply "Fan broken").
  • Attach clear photos when physical equipment (water coolers, electrical switches, projector ports) requires repair.
  • Maintain polite, constructive academic language. Defamatory or inappropriate submissions are rejected by administrators.
