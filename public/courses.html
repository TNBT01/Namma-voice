Affiliated Undergraduate Degrees

Approved Degree Programs

APS College of Arts and Science strictly conducts the following three curriculum streams:

BCA

Bachelor of Computer Applications

Duration: 3 Years (6 Semesters)

A comprehensive curriculum in core computer science, modern software engineering, web application development, database management systems (DBMS), data structures, operating systems, and computing practicals. Designed to equip students for careers in software development and technology.

Core Subjects:
Web Programming DBMS Data Structures Operating Systems Software Engineering

B.Sc

Bachelor of Science

Duration: 3 Years (6 Semesters)

A rigorous program emphasizing scientific enquiry, laboratory experimentation, and analytical problem-solving. Combines core sciences including mathematics, physics, Chemistry and Computer Scienceto build strong theoretical and applied foundations.

Core Subjects:
Physics Mathematics Chemistry Computer Science
View B.Sc Question Papers →
B.A

Bachelor of Arts

Duration: 3 Years (6 Semesters)

A diverse humanities curriculum fostering critical thinking, historical perspective, socioeconomic analysis, language communication, and political awareness. Prepares students for public administration, civil services, academic research, and journalism.

Core Subjects:
Economics History Political Science

Official College Note on Offered Degrees

APS College of Arts and Science is an established institution offering exclusively three degree streams: Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA), Bachelor of Science (B.Sc), and Bachelor of Arts (B.A). All examination question papers, syllabus archives, and departmental allocations within Namma Voice strictly align with these three academic departments.
